## Queue isolation and runtime mutation

- Independent queue isolation passed over approximately 15 seconds wall time while the slow branch drained. The stream receiver observed all 251 decoded handoffs (about 5 seconds of stream content) while the one-second-per-buffer leaky slow branch handled 13; sender and receiver exited 0. This is not 15 seconds of stream endurance.
- Runtime Opus mutation passed in a 4.744-second programmable PyGObject spike. The sequence 128000 -> 192000 -> 64000 -> 96000 -> 128000 bit/s produced 126 received buffers, monotonic PTS with a 20 ms maximum gap, no bus errors and no unexpected EOS.
- These utilities are evidence tooling, not production media components.

## Limitations and open acceptance

- Runs were approximately five seconds; endurance is untested.
- Reconnect, receiver/network interruption, packet loss and SRT statistics under impairment were not tested.
- Stream IDs and SRT encryption/passphrases were not used; direct connection identity and transport statistics were not retained.
- Recording, rotation/finalization and simultaneous recording/monitoring were not tested.
- Every finite GStreamer listener logged an SRT warning when the caller intentionally closed; each still exited 0 after EOS.
- Each LAN sender and the queue/mutation sender emitted two non-fatal `gst_buffer_list_foreach` critical assertions under GStreamer 1.26.2. No bus error, nonzero exit, missing decoded stream or PTS discontinuity was observed; the cause remains open.
- GStreamer receivers used `fakesink`, and container FFplay used dummy audio. Decoding was demonstrated; audible quality was not.
- The iMM-6C paths are mono-to-stereo conversions, not native stereo capture.
- Other boards, all 64-bit targets and Bookworm remain untested.
- Power supply, powered hub, ambient conditions and receiver hardware were not recorded.

## Conclusion

The three exact PCM, FLAC and Opus representations passed finite GStreamer sender/receiver runs, physical iMM-6C ALSA capture with explicit mono-to-stereo conversion, and cached-container FFplay interoperability. Queue isolation and runtime Opus mutation also passed their short Step 1 procedures. This evidence is sufficient to begin Step 2 implementation of only those representations in a programmable PyGObject media process. It is not production acceptance or a blanket Zero W claim; the matrix label remains **Likely compatible / untested**.
